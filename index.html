About us

Cafe Venezia is a family-run café on Chaseville Parade in Winchmore Hill. We serve freshly cooked breakfasts, sandwiches and toasties, hot meals, coffee and tea to our local community, and we’re always happy to see a familiar face. Pop in for a sit-down breakfast, grab a coffee on the way to work, or order online for delivery.
